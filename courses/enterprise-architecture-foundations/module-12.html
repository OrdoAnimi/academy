Module 12 — Mock EA Interview and Final Assessment

Theory 20 minutes + Workshop 60 minutes

Duration: 80 minutes. Artefact produced: EA competency assessment pack.

Module Overview

What you will learn

Defend decisions

Explain the logic behind your architecture choices under questioning.

Use evidence

Refer to artefacts, trade-offs, and assumptions without hiding behind documentation.

Handle challenge

Respond to objections from finance, security, delivery, operations, and executive stakeholders.

Close the course

Assemble a portfolio that demonstrates applied enterprise architecture capability.

Theory

Architecture capability is tested in the room

The final assessment is not a memory test. It tests whether you can explain architecture decisions, defend trade-offs, adapt language to stakeholders, and connect artefacts to executable outcomes. A good architect can show the work, but also explain why the work matters.

Workshop

Run the mock panel

Use the artefacts from the course to answer a mock enterprise architecture interview and final review panel.

Workshop task

Prepare a ten-minute architecture defence using the course artefact set.

Assessment prompt

Walk the panel through the architecture problem, the selected approach, the trade-offs, the roadmap, and the executive decision required.

What to submit

Final artefact pack; ten-minute spoken defence; decision summary; lessons learned; next development plan.
