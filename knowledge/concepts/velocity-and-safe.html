Velocity and SAFe

Where architecture meets scaled delivery.

SAFe helps organise scaled agile delivery. Velocity helps make the architectural decisions underneath delivery visible, owned, traceable, and executable.

Concept Note

Why this comparison matters

Scaled delivery needs more than ceremonies and planning cadence. It needs a visible decision layer: intent, ownership, guardrails, exceptions, evidence, and feedback.

What SAFe is useful for

Reference

SAFe provides a language for organising portfolio, value streams, planning, teams, and delivery cadence at scale.

What Velocity adds

Decision Layer

Velocity focuses on the decision infrastructure underneath delivery: authority, traceability, guardrails, compliant paths, and feedback.

Where they can sit together

Complement

Velocity does not replace scaled delivery frameworks. It can sit beside them as the architecture decision layer.
