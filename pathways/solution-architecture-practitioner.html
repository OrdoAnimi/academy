Solution Architecture Practitioner

From solution context to board-ready delivery guardrails.

For solution architects, delivery architects, cloud advisors, and senior engineers who need to frame options, make risks visible, and support delivery teams with defensible architecture decisions.

Pathway Detail

Audience, outcomes, and prerequisites

Target audience

Solution architects, cloud solution advisors, technical leads moving into architecture, and practitioners preparing for design review responsibilities.

Outcomes

  • Frame a solution context and decision scope.
  • Produce options with trade-offs and risk visibility.
  • Prepare a concise architecture board pack.
  • Connect delivery guardrails to project execution.

Prerequisites

Working delivery experience, basic cloud and integration literacy, and comfort reading technical diagrams or architecture decision records.

Learning Flow

Recommended modules and exercises

Recommended modules

  • Velocity™ scoping and solution context
  • Decision-first options paper
  • Risk, constraints, and assumptions
  • Architecture board pack preparation
  • Delivery guardrails and exception handling

Practice exercises

  • Convert a project brief into architecture decision scope.
  • Draft three viable solution options with trade-offs.
  • Build a board pack for a review forum.
  • Write guardrails for delivery teams and vendors.

Artefacts produced

  • Solution context brief
  • Options paper
  • Architecture decision log
  • Board pack
  • Risk and exception register
