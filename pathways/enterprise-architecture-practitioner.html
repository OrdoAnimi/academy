Enterprise Architecture Practitioner

From capability intent to transformation decisions.

For enterprise architects, transformation advisors, portfolio architects, and emerging Design Authorities who need to connect strategy, capability, investment, roadmap, and governance rhythm.

Pathway Detail

Audience, outcomes, and prerequisites

Target audience

Enterprise architects, senior solution architects moving upstream, portfolio leaders, transformation advisors, and architecture governance leads.

Outcomes

  • Translate strategy into capability and architecture intent.
  • Assess baseline, target, and gap evidence.
  • Shape roadmap options and investment decisions.
  • Operate governance through decision cadence and evidence.

Prerequisites

Experience with enterprise change, delivery portfolios, stakeholder engagement, and architecture artefacts such as visions, roadmaps, capability maps, or decision logs.

Learning Flow

Recommended modules and exercises

Recommended modules

  • Architecture vision and scope
  • BDAT baseline and gap analysis
  • Capability mapping and stakeholder story
  • Options paper and migration roadmap
  • Executive architecture pack

Practice exercises

  • Write a one-page Architecture Vision for a portfolio decision.
  • Map a capability to current and target architecture evidence.
  • Create roadmap options for a constrained transformation scenario.
  • Prepare an executive decision pack.

Artefacts produced

  • Architecture Vision
  • BDAT baseline and gap analysis
  • Capability map
  • Migration roadmap
  • Executive architecture pack
