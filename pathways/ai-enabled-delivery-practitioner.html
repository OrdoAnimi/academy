AI-Enabled Delivery Practitioner

Use AI to accelerate delivery without losing governance.

For architects, business analysts, project practitioners, and delivery leads using AI-assisted workflows to produce better artefacts, decisions, and review evidence.

Pathway Detail

Audience, outcomes, and prerequisites

Target audience

AI-enabled delivery practitioners, architecture analysts, project and program practitioners, advisors, and technologists adopting AI-supported delivery methods.

Outcomes

  • Use AI to draft artefacts from clear intent and context.
  • Review generated work against enterprise criteria.
  • Maintain traceability from prompt to output to decision.
  • Connect AI-assisted work into governance and lifecycle workflows.

Prerequisites

Delivery experience, basic prompt literacy, and willingness to keep human review, source context, and decision accountability visible.

Learning Flow

Recommended modules and exercises

Recommended modules

  • Prompting for architecture intent
  • AI-assisted artefact generation
  • Human review and evidence control
  • Traceability and decision records
  • AI-enabled governance workflows

Practice exercises

  • Turn a messy brief into a structured prompt and context pack.
  • Generate a draft BA, SA, or PM artefact and review it against criteria.
  • Create a prompt record and review note.
  • Route the artefact into a governance lifecycle scenario.

Artefacts produced

  • Prompt context pack
  • Generated artefact draft
  • Human review record
  • Decision traceability note
  • Governance handoff summary
